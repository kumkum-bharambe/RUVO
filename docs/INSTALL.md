# Installing Ruvo on an Android phone

About five minutes. No computer, no account, no technical knowledge needed. Ruvo is free and is not on the Play
Store, so Android asks two extra questions the first time. Both are normal for apps shared this way.

**You need:** an Android phone (Android 8 or newer, almost every phone from 2018 on), about 100 MB free space, and
internet for the download only. After that Ruvo works offline.

## Step 1 · Download

1. On the phone, open this link in **Chrome** (or scan the QR code on the project page):
   **https://github.com/kumkum-bharambe/RUVO/releases/latest/download/Ruvo.apk**
2. Chrome may say *"This type of file can harm your device"* or *"File might be harmful"*. Tap **Download anyway**.
   This is Chrome's standard message for every app file from a website.
3. Wait for the download to finish (about 42 MB). A bar at the bottom says **Ruvo.apk · Open**.

## Step 2 · Allow the install (first time only)

4. Tap **Open**.
5. Android says *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*.
   Tap **Settings**, switch on **Allow from this source**, then press the **Back** arrow.
   (This only lets Chrome offer you app files. You can switch it off again after installing.)

## Step 3 · Install

6. Tap **Install**.
7. Google Play Protect may show *"Unsafe app blocked"* or *"App not recognised"*, because Ruvo is not from the Play
   Store and its developer is a student, not a registered company. Tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**.
   (If you see *"Send app for scanning?"*, you can tap either option.)
8. When it says **App installed**, tap **Open**.

## Step 4 · First open

9. A golden coin spins and the Ruvo logo appears. Tap anywhere to skip.
10. **Create your account.** Type any name, a 10-digit mobile number (starting with 6, 7, 8 or 9) and a 4-digit
    PIN, then type the PIN again. All of this stays on the phone; Ruvo has no server and sends nothing anywhere.
    A made-up number such as 9876543210 is fine.
11. Tap **Explore with a sample student** to see Ruvo filled with a made-up student's money, or
    **Start with my money** to answer five quick questions about your own.

That's it. The [user guide](USER_GUIDE.md) explains every screen; the [testing guide](TESTING.md) lists what to try.

## Removing Ruvo

Press and hold the Ruvo icon, tap **Uninstall** (or drag it to **Uninstall**). This deletes the app and everything in
it. You can also switch **Allow from this source** off again in **Settings → Apps → Chrome → Install unknown apps**.
