# Ruvo user guide

Ruvo shows how many **days** your money will last. This guide walks through every screen. If you chose
**Explore with a sample student**, all the numbers belong to a made-up student called Aarav.

## The idea in one minute

- **Runway** — how many days your free money lasts at the way you usually spend.
- **Next money** — when your next income arrives (pocket money, stipend, salary). If your runway is longer, you
  make it with days to spare; if shorter, you are some days short.
- **Three buckets** — every rupee you have sits in one of them:
  - **Fixed** (purple): already promised, such as rent, mess fees or a phone recharge due before your next money.
  - **Fuel** (yellow): free to spend.
  - **Future** (orange): protected for your goals. Ruvo never counts it as spendable.

## Moving around

The dark bar at the bottom has four tabs: **Home**, **Money**, **Goals**, **Guide**. The round, colourful
**pet** above it is Ruvo:

- **Tap the pet** to ask a question about your money.
- **Drag the pet down** to add a spend quickly.
- The pet's mood follows your money. It is never sad; when money is tight it turns supportive.

The round **+** button at the top of Home and Money also adds a payment.

## Home

Top to bottom:

1. **Greeting**, the **+** button and your initial (opens your **Profile**). "Sample student · switch" lets you pick
   one of five made-up students.
2. **Number chips** that slide across: runway, money available, protected, coming up.
3. **Runway card** — the big number of days, the next-money line (red when short), a timeline from today to your
   next money, **Why N days?** (the whole calculation) and **Let's plan together** (asks Ruvo).
4. **Fixed · Fuel · Future** cards. Tap one to see it in detail.
5. **New payments found** (sample student) — swipe through new payments: right to confirm, left to ignore, up if it
   was shared.
6. **Ruvo noticed** — one observation about your spending, with **See why** or **Not now**.
7. **Daily spending** — the last 7 days as bars. Tap a bar to see that day's payments.
8. **Coming up** — the next bills and their dates.
9. **Before you buy** (blue) — type a price and see what it costs in days, and your runway before and after.
   Ruvo never blocks a purchase; the choice is yours.
10. **A small idea** — a short lesson from the Guide.
11. **Marketplace** (yellow) — one student offer.
12. **Recent** — your latest payments. Tap one to change its category or bucket, split it, or delete it.

## Money

- **You have** card — your balance split into In goals, Planned and Yours to use. Tap it to update your balance.
- Four views:
  - **Overview** — where your money went in the last 30 days, your **Fixed** costs with **Paid** / **Unpaid**
    (tap Unpaid to mark it paid), **+ Add a fixed cost**, then Fuel and Future.
  - **Transactions** — Weekly, Monthly or All; money in and out for the period; every payment by day.
  - **History** — received and spent per month as a chart; tap a month; **Edit** corrects a month's totals.
  - **Owed** — money friends owe you from split payments; **Send UPI link** or **They paid**.

## Adding a payment

Tap **+** or drag the pet down. Choose **Spent** or **Received**, type the amount, optionally what it was, pick
**Fixed** or **Fuel**, switch on **Shared with friends?** to split it, then tap a category to save. Your runway
updates straight away. **+ New category** makes your own.

## Goals

Each goal has a picture, the amount saved out of the target, a progress bar with milestones, and the date.
**+ ₹100** moves ₹100 from Fuel into the goal. Tap a goal to pour more in or move money back. **New goal** asks a
name, a target, an optional monthly amount and a date (3, 6 or 12 months, or pick a date). **Goal insights**
says when the goal will land at your current pace.

## Guide

- **See the list** shows all 36 books. The ticks fill in as you read; there are no streaks and no pressure.
- **Marketplace** — real student offers. Tap **How to get it** on an offer for the steps and a button to the
  brand's own app or site. Tap the bookmark to save an offer; **Saved** shows only saved offers.
- **New this week**, **Picked for you** and shelves: Money basics, Save better, Spend better, Student money,
  Stay safe on UPI, Credit and borrowing, First salary and tax, Investing basics.
- **Inside a book**: swipe or tap **Next**. Pages include the idea, a real student situation, why it matters, a
  rule of thumb or fact, sometimes a choose-your-path story or a calculator, a short quiz, key takeaways and a
  **Do it now** button. Underlined words explain themselves when tapped. The speaker button reads the page aloud.

## Ask Ruvo

Tap the pet. Pick a suggested question or type your own, for example "Why this many days?", "How much can I
spend today?" or "Can I afford ₹1,500?". Answers use your own numbers and work offline. The language button (**English**) switches the chat to Hindi; **Listen** reads an answer aloud. Ruvo explains; it never recommends investments or loans.

## Profile and Settings

Tap your initial on Home. **Profile** shows your runway, goals, books read and this month's money. **Settings**:
light (**Daybreak**) or dark (**Night swim**) look, your data (switch sample student, or **Use my own** to start
fresh), spoken replies, notifications, hide my balance, your UPI ID for split reminders, export your data, clear
transactions or delete everything. **Change PIN** and **Sign out** are on Profile.

## Outside the app

- **Home-screen widget** shows your runway: press and hold the home screen → Widgets → Ruvo.
- **Shortcuts**: press and hold the Ruvo icon for quick actions.
